Connect over Wi-Fi

Enter your Android device IP address and port

  1. Enable Developer options (tap Build number 7 times)
  2. Turn on USB debugging
  3. Connect your phone and accept the prompt

Tip: plug the phone in by USB once, open Devices and click “Switch to Wi-Fi” — the address fills in automatically next time.

Connect over USB

Plug in your phone — it appears in Connected Devices automatically

  1. Enable Developer options (tap Build number 7 times)
  2. Turn on USB debugging
  3. Plug in the cable and tap “Allow” on the phone

Phone shows “Waiting for approval”? Check the phone screen for the USB debugging prompt.
